OtakuVerse

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

OtakuVerse

This name was chosen because it blends "otaku" (a passionate fan of anime and games) with "universe," reflecting a hub that brings together anime and video game fandoms in one place. It is short, easy to remember, and clearly signals the site's focus to visitors.

Optional domain availability: otakuverse.io

2. Site Purpose

OtakuVerse is a content and community hub for fans of anime and video games. The site provides:

  • Curated reviews and recommendations for ongoing and upcoming anime series.
  • Guides and recommendations for video games connected to anime franchises, including adaptations and anime-inspired RPGs.
  • A community space where fans can discuss favorite series, games, and crossovers.

3. Scenarios

  • I just finished a great isekai anime — what similar RPG video games would I enjoy?
  • Where can I find honest reviews of this season's new anime releases before I commit to watching?
  • How do I connect with other fans who want to discuss a specific game or anime crossover?

4. Color Schema

The color schema uses a dark "gamer" background with neon accents to reflect the energy of anime and gaming culture.

  • Deep Space Navy (#12121a) — used as the main page background.
  • Neon Violet (#8b5cf6) — used for headings and navigation highlights.
  • Cyber Cyan (#22d3ee) — used for links, buttons, and accents.
  • Soft White (#f4f4f8) — used for body text for readability.

5. Typography

Two fonts from Google Fonts were selected:

  • Orbitron — a bold, futuristic display font used for all headings and navigation, matching the gaming/anime aesthetic.
  • Poppins — a clean, modern sans-serif used for body text, lists, and general content for easy reading.
